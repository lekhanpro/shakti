\section{Emergency activation and response}

The \term{panic controller} owns one incident lifecycle: idle, countdown, active, then resolved or false alarm. Multiple triggers during one active incident must not create duplicate emergencies. Each dispatch channel has its own timeout, result and retry policy.

\subsection{Dispatch sequence and recovery}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Persist.} Write the incident locally before remote work. Load active contacts and a cached location. If storage fails, report the error and still attempt SMS; do not let a failed log suppress a help request.
\item \textbf{Send.} Hand carrier SMS to every active contact without waiting for internet, reverse geocoding or a fresh GPS fix. Use the last known fix with its age, otherwise say location unavailable; include the tracker link. A fresh fix can follow later. This ordering is necessary to meet the latency target.
\item \textbf{Run isolated channels.} In parallel, start permitted 60 s audio recording, audible deterrence when enabled, and cloud dispatch/Telegram when online. An error in one channel must not stop the others. Show pending, handed off, failed or queued truthfully.
\item \textbf{Track.} Save breadcrumbs every 10 s while active; upload through the queue. Display last-update age and location accuracy. After process restart, restore the open incident and resume tracking without re-sending every initial alert.
\item \textbf{Resolve.} Verify the real PIN, stop sensors/recording/deterrence, persist the final status and queue synchronization. For ordinary incidents, send an ``I'm safe'' update; a covert incident must avoid an unexpected revealing notification. A wrong PIN cannot dismiss the SOS.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{cautionbox}[title=Failure cases must be visible]
No SIM, permission denial, missing contacts or no signal must produce a clear channel result. A missing GPS fix must not block SMS. During an outage, retain local evidence and queue cloud work. Do not label carrier handoff as confirmed delivery or automatically resend an ambiguous SMS result.
\end{cautionbox}
