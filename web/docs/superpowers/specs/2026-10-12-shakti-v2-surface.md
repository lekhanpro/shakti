# Shakti V2 landing surface

Mode: Persuade, with interactive product proof. Audience: young women on Android first, with guardians as a secondary audience. Job: understand what happens after an SOS and try the trigger/tracker demos.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
Shakti is a safety network that answers. The surface is a motion-graphic sequence rather than a conventional landing page: large type, full-color chapter cuts, a luminous signal object, a scroll-choreographed city, and one concrete interaction per act.

### OWN-WORLD
Pulse Motion uses black and bone as the base, electric violet as the product signal, acid-lime for ready/confirmed states, and coral only for urgent help. Sections are poster-like and mostly sharp-edged, with pill controls, giant Bricolage Grotesque headlines, mono timing labels, orbital diagrams, and a 2.5D luminous signal field. The visual vocabulary comes from motion-design studies: easing, offset, masking, parallax, and scale.

### STORY
The visitor enters through a signal field and oversized “Three presses. A whole network.” statement. A bone manifesto names the emotional job. The scroll story shows walk, trigger, and guardian response. Violet stages the working trigger, acid-lime stages the guardian network, bone stages nearby help and project breadth, black stages the AI/privacy material, and coral ends with direct helplines. The primary action is still to try the trigger.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
The header is a minimal fixed black rail. The first viewport is a nearly full-screen black field with a giant low-opacity SHAKTI word behind it. On the left, a compact chapter label, oversized three-press thesis, and one acid-lime action. On the right, a tall signal field with a violet orb, elliptical rings, animated ribbon, depth particles, crosshairs, and a ready-state readout. A small bottom rail says to scroll into the response.

### FORM
This is a code-led Pulse Motion direction, ranked first for distinctiveness while preserving product clarity. The signature interaction is the page-wide chapter choreography: the hero signal field, bone manifesto break, sticky three-scene city sequence, violet trigger object, and acid network field. Seed key: shakti-pulse-motion-code.
